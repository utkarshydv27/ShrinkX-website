## 🚀 Why ShrinkX?

- **Private by design** — files never leave your device, everything runs client-side in the browser
- **No sign-up** — no account, no email, no limits
- **Fast** — instant processing, no server round-trips
- **Free forever** — no hidden pricing, no watermarks
- **Mobile friendly** — works smoothly on phones and tablets

---

## 💡 Use cases

- Compressing a photo to fit a government form's file-size limit
- Shrinking a PDF before emailing it
- Making a single PDF out of scanned pages
- Cropping a profile picture to the right aspect ratio
- Building a collage for social media
